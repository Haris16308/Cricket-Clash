ABOUT THE PROJECT

Beyond The Boundary

Every player has a story. Every stat tells it.

Cricket Clash is a cricket player comparison website designed for cricket fans. It allows users to compare two players side by side and explore their performance through statistics such as runs, batting average, strike rate, centuries, and matches played. Our goal is to make cricket statistics easy to understand and help fans explore player performances in a simple and interactive way.
